Projects

deepC · repo

A deep learning engine in modern C++ with zero external dependencies and no AI codegen: reverse-mode automatic differentiation over an arena-owned expression graph, topological backward scheduling via a pending-consumer counter, and a two-layer design that isolates raw-pointer compute kernels from the graph/ownership layer, so the CPU backend swaps to hand-written CUDA kernels with no engine changes (RTX 5070 port in progress).

  • Trained an MLP (784→128→10) on MNIST end to end on the engine: 95.85% test accuracy, every operator gradcheck-certified both raw and composed through the full network.
  • The finite-difference gradient checker is derived, not borrowed: I computed the truncation-vs-cancellation error floor analytically (~3e-11 at h≈1e-5) and matched it to measurement. The harness has caught three classes of silent-wrongness bugs, including one in itself.
  • Measured a Debug→Release speedup asymmetry of 9.4× (inference) vs 2.4× (training) and traced it to memory access patterns in the backward pass.
  • In progress: CUDA kernels verified against the CPU implementations as oracles, and a small language model trained on the engine.

Extropic DTM energy-claim audit · repo

Extropic's Denoising Thermodynamic Model paper (arXiv:2510.23972) asserts a K_mix = 250 Gibbs-sweep constant at the center of its energy-efficiency claim. I replicated the Fashion-MNIST model on the official thrml stack and measured the FID-vs-K quality curve the paper does not report.

  • The fixed-β regime the energy model assumes stalls roughly 13 FID short of the quality plateau at every budget tested (K ≤ 3200), implying a 3.2× correction to the energy claim at matched quality.
  • Measured a 0.30–0.32 J/image GPU baseline against the paper's projected 23 nJ.
  • Surfaced two reproducibility defects in the vendor stack: an un-checkpointed annealing parameter, and a load path that silently samples an untrained model. Full report in the repo.

PlayFormer · repo

A 2.48M-parameter Transformer encoder for NFL play-type prediction, trained over 709,000 plays from 19 seasons of nflfastR data with a dual-head factorization separating rare-event prediction from the pass-vs-run decision.

  • 0.862 test macro-F1 (2-model ensemble) on a held-out 2024 season under a strict temporal split: predict next season, not interpolate within seen ones.
  • 160+ automated experiments run through a custom optimization loop, each logged with a keep/discard verdict.

Research

Domain-Specificity of Refusal Representations in Large Language Models · PDF

C. Tucker, M. Leon. Proceedings of FLAIRS-39, May 2026. Best Poster Award, 1 of 3 conference-wide.

Is the single "refusal direction" in LLM activation space domain-specific or universal? We extracted per-domain and universal refusal directions from Llama 3.1 8B residual-stream activations and ran cross-domain directional ablation with LLM-judge scoring and random-direction controls. Refusal geometry turned out to be domain-invariant: within- and cross-domain ablation were statistically indistinguishable (25.6% vs 32.2% refusal, Fisher's exact p = 0.324), and every domain direction aligned strongly with the universal one (mean cosine 0.916). Removing one direction compromises guardrails in every domain at once.

Auditing Abliteration: Normalization Placement, Edit Budget, and Transfer in Single-Direction Refusal Removal

C. Tucker, M. Leon. IEEE Opportunity Research Scholars Symposium (ORSS), 2026. Accepted, to appear in IEEE Xplore.

The follow-on: if one direction carries refusal, how robust are models to its deliberate removal? We audit weight-space refusal removal as a tampering attack across Qwen-2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, and Gemma-2-9B, and isolate three methodological factors an evaluation must hold constant to rank model robustness. Reproducibility artifacts released with a dual-use-aware withholding policy.
